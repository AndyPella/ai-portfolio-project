# Decision 001 — Use Two Connected Demonstrations

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** 2026-08-10

## Question

What form should the AI portfolio demonstration take?

## Facts

- The portfolio must support job applications and remain NDA-safe.
- It should demonstrate both a working AI-enabled product and understanding of how and why it was built.
- Combining the complete development story and working product into one experience would dilute both.

## Analysis

A single demonstration would carry two different audience goals: proving that the product works and proving disciplined product reasoning. Separate but linked demonstrations give each goal a clear narrative.

## Decision

Create:

1. Demo 1 — the AI-assisted research, design, build, testing, and decision process.
2. Demo 2 — the resulting functional end-to-end product.

## Rationale

The two demonstrations can stand independently while providing evidence for each other. Demo 1 explains important product choices; Demo 2 shows those choices operating in practice.

## Effect on scope

- Capture consequential decisions and evidence throughout development.
- Link decisions in Demo 1 to observable behavior in Demo 2.
- Keep the employer-facing build story curated rather than presenting raw history.

## Validation

A hiring manager should be able to understand either demonstration independently and trace an important documented decision to its implementation.
