# Routine availability and rental progression

Approved October 12, 2026. Check requested availability and Proceed to rent are routine actions qualified by the current workflow. They advance directly without asking the worker for verification evidence or an additional confirmation dialog.

Checking availability still validates the request and reevaluates equipment. Proceeding still applies the temporary-allocation rules and only advances to customer identification after a successful allocation. Invalid requests, blocked equipment, conflicts, and authority restrictions remain enforced.

The existing domain command record receives a clearly labeled UI interaction reference for these two actions, not invented inspection, approval, or customer verification evidence. Staff approval and qualified resolution actions retain their explicit evidence prompts. This change supersedes the earlier blanket action-dialog presentation for these two routine controls.

Browser validation checks both actions produce no dialog, protection is recorded before intake, and staff approval still requires evidence. Existing rental and role checks remain covered.
