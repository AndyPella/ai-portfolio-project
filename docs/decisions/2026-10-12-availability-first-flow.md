# Availability-first Customer Service flow

Approved October 12, 2026. This supersedes the Customer Service starting order in the earlier rental-workspace UI decision: availability precedes customer identification.

1. Gather equipment needs, rental dates and expected use; evaluate equipment availability, readiness, blockers and conditions.
2. Offer a choice: finish an availability-only inquiry, or proceed to rent.
3. On proceeding, apply the agreed four-hour temporary protection before completing customer identification. Branch into an existing customer match or the assumed working new-customer process.
4. Complete required customer information and checks, then prepare the rental agreement for staff approval and customer acceptance.

Availability-only inquiries require neither a customer profile nor an allocation. Customer Service begins on Equipment availability; selecting another sample request returns to that section. Customer identification and agreement navigation become available after successful protection. Expiry and renewed approval rules continue to apply. The existing/new paths represent sample data and required checks; no CRM search or registration integration is built.

The shared summary identifies an availability inquiry rather than presenting a sample customer before the rental path begins. Dates and expected use can be reviewed and updated in the availability section. Sample requests still supply fictional defaults. Role-based resolution remains available for blockers; blocked equipment cannot proceed directly to rental protection.

Validation includes the initial availability section, availability-only completion, protection before intake, existing/new route selection, retained protection during intake, role and section context, equipment search/list consistency, service conditions, hold resolution, expiry and approval gates, and desktop/mobile layouts. Build/type checks, lint and browser smoke checks passed; no domain rules or known-bug fixes changed.
