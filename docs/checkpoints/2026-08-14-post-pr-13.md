### Equipment Readiness engine

PR #13 added a deterministic engine that evaluates:

- Required inspection presence and chronology
- Explicit inspection result
- Inspection expiration and operating-hour limits
- Maintenance due dates and operating-hour triggers
- Blocking maintenance and repair conditions
- Defects and explicitly linked corrective work
- Asset qualification and required attachments
- Supporting evidence references
- Contradictory, adverse, pending, required, unresolved, or unrecognized equipment states

The engine:

- Requires an explicit `YYYY-MM-DD` as-of date.
- Excludes future-dated evidence from an as-of evaluation.
- Does not use system time.
- Does not allow source status alone to establish readiness.
- Uses fail-closed reconciliation: uncertainty or unresolved conflict produces `Human Review Required`.
- Returns structured outcomes, reason codes, explanations, and evidence references.
- Does not mutate the snapshot or connect to the private Sheet.

## Current Architecture and Boundaries

### Equipment Readiness

Equipment Readiness answers:

> Is this equipment operationally ready based on the evidence available as of a specified date?

It evaluates inspection, maintenance, defect, qualification, attachment, and evidence controls.

### Rental Availability

Rental Availability answers:

> Is operationally ready equipment available for a particular rental request and time window?

Rental Availability is intentionally separate and has not yet been implemented.

### Explicit exclusions from the completed readiness increment

- Reservation creation, modification, confirmation, cancellation, or fulfillment
- Reservation-conflict evaluation
- Rental-date and buffer calculations
- Alternative-date ranking
- Customer relationship management
- Parts ordering or inventory management
- Full maintenance scheduling
- Live system integrations
- Acquisition-system migration workflows
- Adding new equipment inventory

## Recorded Operating Decisions

- The demo must remain short enough for independent review and public portfolio use.
- Operational context is demonstrated through controlled decisions, evidence, dependencies, and human gates.
- AI assistance must not replace deterministic business controls.
- Human review is required when evidence is missing, contradictory, unresolved, or unrecognized.
- Equipment Readiness and Rental Availability remain separate capabilities.
- The Google Sheet is the editable source; the committed JSON file is the reviewed runtime snapshot.
- Data changes begin in the Sheet, refresh the JSON snapshot, and pass validation and diff review before application use.
- Customer information remains a separate mock view and does not expand into a CRM demonstration.
- System integrations and M&A integration workflows are reserved for a later demonstration.
- Ask before creating any file intended for direct download to local system storage.

## Next Increment

Build a small, read-only Rental Availability evaluator for the Customer Service workflow.

The first scenario should:

1. Accept an equipment request or recognized Equipment ID.
2. Use the user's assumed current location unless another location is supplied.
3. Require the selected equipment to pass Equipment Readiness.
4. Evaluate the requested rental period and agreed preparation buffer.
5. Detect overlap with confirmed reservations or operational holds.
6. Return a structured availability outcome with the conflicting reservation or interruption as evidence.
7. Keep all reservation operations read-only.
8. Use the existing reservation-conflict scenario in the static dataset as a regression test.

Initial outcomes should remain limited to:

- `Rental Ready`
- `Rental Ready with Conditions`
- `Not Available`
- `Remediation Required`
- `Human Review Required`

Alternative equipment and ranked alternative dates may follow after the core conflict evaluation is stable.
