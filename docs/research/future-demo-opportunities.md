# Future Demo Opportunities

This document preserves useful follow-on demonstrations without adding them to the
current prototype scope.

## M&A Systems Integration Workflow

- **Status:** Parking lot
- **Priority:** Revisit after the equipment-readiness demonstration is complete

### Opportunity

Demonstrate how the manifold could coordinate the integration of an acquired
equipment-rental business into Northstar Ridge Equipment Group's data and operating
environment.

A future demonstration could cover:

- Identifying acquired systems and data sources
- Mapping equipment, customer, maintenance, inspection, and reservation records
- Choosing migration versus connection strategies
- Validating identifiers and record relationships
- Reconciling missing or conflicting records
- Tracking cutover readiness and exceptions
- Confirming that migrated or connected information is available to business processes
- Transitioning the integration into normal support and operations

### Relationship To The Current Demo

The current equipment-readiness prototype assumes this work has already been
completed. That assumption is recorded as A-002.

The future integration demonstration could produce the connected and reconciled data
that the current workflow consumes.

### Boundary

The future demo should focus on systems-integration workflow and assurance rather than
rebuilding the equipment-readiness decision logic. It should remain fictional,
public-safe, and independent of Salesforce-derived systems, data, or processes.
