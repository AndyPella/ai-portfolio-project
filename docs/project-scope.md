# AI Portfolio Demonstration — Working Scope

**Status:** Discovery  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-10

## Objective

Create an NDA-safe portfolio that demonstrates the ability to frame a business problem, research it, define requirements, design and build an AI-enabled workflow, integrate systems, apply human oversight, measure outcomes, and explain the decisions made.

## Demonstration structure

### Demo 1 — How It Was Built

A curated, evidence-backed account of the real process used to select, design, build, test, and refine Demo 2.

### Demo 2 — Working Product

A functional end-to-end AI-enabled business workflow. The topic has not yet been selected.

## Confirmed decisions

- Create two separate but connected demonstrations.
- Use fictional organizations, people, systems, and data.
- Exclude proprietary, confidential, and NDA-protected information.
- Show meaningful AI-assisted automation, workflow design, integration, analytics, customer experience, human oversight, and responsible controls.
- Preserve authentic decisions, tests, failures, and revisions throughout the project.
- Keep the repository private until a public-release review is complete.

## Proposed, not approved

- A reusable AI workflow orchestration or assurance product.
- One fully implemented representative workflow.
- Visible confidence, tool activity, approvals, exceptions, audit history, and outcome metrics.
- An interactive version of Demo 1.

## Topic-selection criteria

Candidates will be evaluated for:

- Hiring-market relevance
- Clear business and customer value
- A credible end-to-end workflow
- Appropriate, non-superficial AI use
- Integration and human-oversight opportunities
- Realistic fictional data
- Ease of explanation
- Differentiation from common chatbot demonstrations
- Cross-industry transferability
- Feasibility of a credible first release
- Fit with transferable skills without relying on protected work

## Constraints

- Public-safe and NDA-safe
- First presentable version should be achievable quickly
- More than AI-generated text must be demonstrated
- Claims must be supported by visible behavior or labeled as assumptions
- Raw transcripts will not be used as the employer-facing case study
